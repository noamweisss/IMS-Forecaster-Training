Module 1: Aviation Weather Fundamentals & Regulations

Welcome to Module 1
This module forms the foundation of the Aviation Weather Forecasting Certification. It is designed to familiarize professional meteorologists with the international regulatory framework, operational flight planning considerations, airspace structures, and the critical principles of altimetry.

Module Competencies

Upon completion of this module, you will be certified to:

  • Navigate and apply WMO and ICAO regulatory frameworks governing aeronautical meteorology.
  • Integrate meteorological factors into fuel consumption and route optimization strategies.
  • Assess airspace classifications and route structures to support aviation planning.
  • Calculate and apply correct altimetry settings for safe flight operations.

Curriculum Structure

Lesson Estimated Duration Key Focus Areas
1. International Regulatory Frameworks 25 mins WMO vs ICAO roles, Annex 3 compliance, Standards and Recommended Practices (SARPs).
2. Aviation Route Planning & Fuel Management 30 mins Headwinds/Tailwinds impact, temperature effects on performance, fuel contingency planning.
3. Airspace Structure & Classification 20 mins Flight Information Regions (FIRs), Class A-G airspace minimums, Special Use Airspace.
4. Principles of Aviation Altimetry 25 mins QNH, QFE, QNE, Transition Altitudes/Levels, cold weather error calculations.

Module Assessment

Certification Quiz Requirements
To pass this module, you must complete the final assessment with a score of 75% or higher.

The quiz consists of 16 application-level multiple-choice questions. It is designed to test your operational judgment and ability to apply concepts to real-world aviation scenarios, not just rote recall.
