Aviation Weather Forecasting / Module 4

Module 4: Aviation Forecast Products & Warnings

Estimated duration: 90 minutes

Welcome to Module 4. In this final module of the Aviation Weather Forecasting Certification course, we shift our focus from local aerodrome phenomena to the specialized products and global hazards that dictate international aviation routing and safety.

You will learn the philosophy behind writing effective, user-friendly Terminal Aerodrome Forecasts (TAFs) and how to manage complex meteorological transitions using strict change groups. We will also explore the severe hazards posed by Tropical Storms and Volcanic Ash, and how global authorities (VAACs and RSMCs) track these phenomena. Finally, you will learn to decode the World Area Forecast System (WAFS) charts that pilots use for en-route navigation.

Core Competencies:
  • Produce clear, concise, and accurate Terminal Aerodrome Forecasts (TAFs) tailored to operational needs.
  • Apply appropriate change groups (BECMG, TEMPO) and amendment criteria to TAFs.
  • Interpret advisories and track forecasts for tropical cyclones and volcanic ash.
  • Decode global high-level (SWH) and medium-level (SWM) SIGWX charts from World Area Forecast Centres.
