## Lesson 2 — Operational TAF Amendments & Edge Cases

The TAF deck establishes the change groups and the *principle* of amending on a
flight-category change. The **specific ICAO Annex 3 amendment-criteria
thresholds** were added from the standard so the lesson is operationally usable —
please confirm these are the exact values IMS applies:

1. **Visibility amendment thresholds:** 150, 350, 600, 800, 1500, 3000, 5000 m.
2. **Cloud-base (BKN/OVC) amendment thresholds:** 100, 200, 500, 1000, 1500 ft.
3. **Wind amendment criteria:** mean direction change ≥ 60° (mean ≥ 10 kt either
   side); mean-speed change ≥ 10 kt; gust change ≥ 10 kt (mean ≥ 15 kt).
4. **Phenomena onset/cessation** that force an amendment (TS, FZRA, FZDZ, FZFG,
   moderate/heavy precipitation, dust/sandstorm, etc.).
   *Used in:* the "ICAO amendment criteria" table, the worked amendment example,
   the takeaways, and quiz Q05.
