## Lesson 2 — Aircraft Icing Hazards

Almost entirely source-grounded (SLW, rime/glaze/mixed, freezing rain, the
tephigram decision tree on slide 31, WAFS icing products, the AF447 case, and
the "icing above 0°C / carburettor icing" slides). One numeric envelope was
added from standard references — please confirm:

1. **Carburettor-icing temperature envelope** — that induction-air cooling in
   the carburettor (pressure drop + fuel evaporation, on the order of ~20°C) lets
   ice form at outside air temperatures of roughly **+10°C to +25°C** in humid
   air. The deck establishes that icing can occur above 0°C and that carburettor
   icing exists; the specific temperature envelope is the standard-reference part.
   *Used in:* "Beyond the Airframe" section + the takeaways + quiz.
